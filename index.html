RP CS Check

RP CS Check is a private customer-support automation tool operated by Kevin Chan.

It is designed to synchronise authorised Jira support tickets with designated Google Sheets and store ticket attachments in a designated Google Drive folder.

Google access

The tool reads and updates support-ticket spreadsheets, uploads attachments, and organises ticket folders. It runs on the operator's computer and is not offered as a public sign-up service.
