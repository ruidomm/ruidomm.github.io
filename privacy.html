RP CS Check Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

RP CS Check is operated by Me for authorised customer-support administration.

Data accessed and purpose

The tool accesses designated Google Sheets and Drive folders, including support-ticket descriptions, replies, statuses, attachments and file metadata. Tickets may contain player identifiers and other information supplied in support reports.

This information is used to synchronise support cases between Jira and Google Sheets, organise attachments, and track updates without creating duplicate reports or replies.

Storage and sharing

Support information is transferred between the configured Atlassian Jira and Google Workspace resources. Access to those resources is controlled by their existing permissions.

Authentication tokens, synchronisation records, logs and downloaded attachments may be stored on the operator's computer. Credentials and support data are not published on this website.

New or edited game-provider support replies are sent to the OpenAI API for translation into English before being posted to the corresponding Jira ticket. Only the reply text is submitted for translation; it may include player identifiers or other information written in that reply. Ticket attachments and complete conversation histories are not submitted for translation. Translations are cached on the operator's computer to avoid repeat requests. Requests use store=false; this does not itself guarantee zero provider retention. OpenAI processes this information under its applicable API data-handling terms.

Retention and control

Local files remain until removed by the operator. Information stored in Jira and Google Workspace follows the retention and deletion practices of the owners of those resources.

The operator can stop the automation, delete its local files, and revoke its access through Google Account permissions. Revoking access does not delete previously synchronised records.

Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google Workspace API data is not used to develop, improve or train general-purpose or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models.

Contact

For questions or data-handling requests, contact good@gmail.com.
